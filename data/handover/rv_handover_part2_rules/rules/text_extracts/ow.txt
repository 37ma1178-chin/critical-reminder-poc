              Architecture Freeze - Architectures 1 to 20
 Safekeeping baseline - 8 October 2026


 Core invariant: Nothing shown to the user may exist only visually. Every visible or concealed item must correspond to dimensional
 and semantic data in the Canonical RV Model. 2D, 3D, engineering, utilities, costing, validation and fabrication outputs must
 describe the same RV.

 This document records the agreed architecture before implementation. It does not claim that the India-wide regulatory,
 donor-vehicle, component or pricing databases have already been populated or independently verified.

 1. Product & Scope Architecture
 Purpose
 India-specific RV planning, engineering, compliance-assistance, costing, visualization and fabrication-documentation platform.

 Core gates
 Every design is evaluated independently for regulatory support, physical fit, engineering viability, financial fit and
 buildability/serviceability.

 Project paths
 Existing vehicle; buy a complete donor; supported chassis/body-build route; or Help Me Choose. Private and commercial use
 remain distinct.

 Canonical rule
 The Canonical RV Model is the single source of truth. 2D, 3D, templates and AI outputs cannot maintain separate physical realities.

 Budget
 Budget is an input constraint. Requirements are Must Have, Preferred or Optional. Impossible designs are rejected rather than
 visually distorted.



 2. Regulatory & Compliance Intelligence
 Context
 Evaluate exact donor/variant/year, current vehicle state, proposed modification manifest, intended use, jurisdiction and date.

 Hierarchy
 Central rules, technical standards, State/UT overlays, official procedures, vehicle applicability and project compliance are separate
 layers.

 Evidence
 Official statutory and technical evidence outranks procedural, manufacturer, secondary and community evidence. Community
 evidence alone cannot establish legality.

 Statuses
 VERIFIED, CONDITIONAL, PROFESSIONAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED, UNKNOWN, UNSUPPORTED and NON-COMPLIANT
 where evidence supports it.

 Versioning
 Rules, forms and procedures are versioned and superseded, never overwritten. Conflicts are surfaced. AI cannot independently
 declare compliance.



 3. Donor Vehicle Master Database
 Identity
 Manufacturer > family/model > generation > exact variant > model-year/effective range > wheelbase/body configuration >
 seating/body option > relevant powertrain.

 Physical states
 Chassis/cowl, OEM/factory body, registered complete vehicle, stripped conversion shell and completed RV are separate states.

 Core data
 Exterior geometry, wheelbase/tracks/overhangs, GVW, kerb/unladen mass, payload, axle ratings where available, seating/body and
 relevant mechanical data.

 Interior data
 Lengths and widths at multiple heights, headroom, floor height, wheel arches, cab intrusion, openings, pillars, roof curvature,
 luggage/service bays and structural/no-cut zones.

 Provenance
 Every consequential value is official, OEM/body-builder, verified measurement, reputable secondary, community-measured,
 derived, estimated or unknown, with source/applicability/tolerance.

 Support gate
 Only sufficiently complete and evidenced exact variants become design-supported; incomplete records remain concept-only or
 measurement-required.



 4. Vehicle Digital-Twin Architecture
 Coordinates
 One vehicle-fixed XYZ system and defined datums for all surfaces, components and service routes.

 Geometry
 Exterior shape, bare/finished interior, cab, wheel arches, openings, pillars, chassis/structure, mechanical exclusions,
 luggage/service bays, roof/underfloor and protected zones.

 Fidelity
 Taper, curvature, steps, raised floors, wheel housings and cab geometry are preserved; a bus or Traveller is never an engineering
 cuboid.

 Build-up
 Insulation, framing/service voids, floor, ceiling, walls and finishes explicitly reduce usable volume.

 Authority
 Engineering geometry is authoritative; cosmetic meshes cannot redefine dimensions. Project-specific verified measurements may
 refine a project twin without overwriting the master.



 5. Canonical Geometry & Spatial Engine
 Object model
 Every object has transform, physical volume, mounting footprint, operating/motion envelope, service envelope, safety/clearance
 envelope and semantic type.

 Operations
 Move, rotate, align, snap, measure, collision, containment, clearance, swept-volume and zone eligibility.

 Views
 Top, left/right, front/rear, roof, underfloor, longitudinal/cross section, cutaway and 3D are projections of the same canonical
 geometry.

 Integrity
 Engineering units are independent of display scale. Fixed SKUs cannot be shrunk; parametric fabricated objects resize only within
 validated ranges.

 Reliability
 Geometry edits are atomic, versioned and checkpointed.



 6. Component Intelligence Database
 Scope
 Real prefabricated SKUs: TVs, refrigerators, HVAC, batteries, inverter/chargers, solar, pumps, heaters, toilets, sinks, tanks, vents,
 windows, lighting, distribution and connectivity.

 Engineering fields
 Dimensions, mass, cut-outs, mounting, clearances, operating/service envelopes, voltage, power, energy use where known,
 plumbing, ventilation and installation requirements.

 Compatibility
 Geometry, electrical, plumbing, thermal, mounting and serviceability are separate compatibility dimensions.

 Evidence/cost
 OEM manuals/datasheets outrank reseller claims. Price observations are dated vendor/region records. Visual assets never redefine
 engineering envelopes.



 7. Parametric Fabrication Library
 Scope
 Custom seats/recliners where applicable, benches, sofa-beds, beds, dinettes, tables, cabinets, wardrobes, counters, partitions,
 bathroom modules, storage and service housings.

 Parameters
 Fixed values, editable ranges, dependencies, materials/build-ups, hardware and manufacturing constraints.

 Motion/ergonomics
 Fold, slide, recline, drawers and doors include swept volumes; objects expose seat pitch, legroom, bed size and access
 requirements.

 Rule
 Appearance is separate from engineering geometry; resizing is allowed only inside manufacturable and ergonomic limits.



 8. Hidden Services / MEP & Utilities
 Principle
 Reserve concealed service volume before allocating all space to furniture.

 Electrical
 Generation/source, battery, protection, busbars, inverter/charger, AC/DC distribution, circuits, loads, solar, shore and
 alternator/other charging form a connected graph.

 Water
 Fill, fresh tank, pump, fixtures, drains, grey tank, toilet/black-water, vents and service/drain points form a connected graph.

 Routing
 Cables, pipes and ducts consume real space and obey structural, heat, motion, bend and incompatibility rules.

 Serviceability
 Normal cabin views may hide systems, but engineering layers reveal exact locations. Maintainable equipment requires
 access/removal space.



 9. Engineering Constraint & Safety Engine
 Mass
 Evaluate dry, travel and maximum-use states. Components carry mass and XYZ location; distribution/axle effects are calculated
 where donor data permits.

 Spatial
 Check circulation, exits, furniture/recliner motion, appliance doors, headroom, human access and service clearances.

 Utilities
 Check electrical supply/demand, charging, solar area, water/tank capacity, plumbing feasibility and HVAC dependencies.

 Structure/severity
 Enforce protected zones and escalate unresolved structural questions. Findings are Critical, Major, Moderate or Minor.

 Missing engineering evidence never becomes PASS; every failure is explainable with actual/required values and remediation.



 10. Requirements, Onboarding & Project Intent
 Flow
 Existing/buy/chassis/help-me-choose > jurisdiction > private/commercial > budget > occupancy/use > features > autonomy > style.

 Domains
 Travel seats, berths, trip duration, climate, comfort, bathroom, kitchen, appliances, storage, HVAC, water, electrical/solar,
 accessibility, entertainment and special requirements.

 Priorities
 Must Have, Preferred and Optional. Adaptive questioning suppresses irrelevant questions and exposes material
 assumptions/defaults.

 Output
 A deterministic Project Intent object consumed by regulation, donor recommendation, templates, solver and costing.



 11. Budget, Cost & Commercial Estimation
 Cost stack
 Donor/chassis, body construction, stripping, structure, insulation/shell, furniture/seating, electrical/solar, HVAC, plumbing/tanks/WC,
 appliances, finishes, labour, applicable documentation/testing, logistics and contingency.

 States
 Estimated, quoted, committed and actual costs remain separate with date, geography, tax basis and source.

 Optimization
 When over budget, preserve Must Haves and offer explicit savings from Preferred/Optional choices with their engineering
 consequences.

 Uncertainty
 Early costs are ranges/confidence bands rather than false precision; changes propagate into affected cost lines.



 12. Verified Layout Template Library
 Definition
 Exact donor/twin version plus visible layout, seating, furniture, appliances, batteries/inverter, tanks, electrical/plumbing/HVAC,
 roof/underfloor, storage, mass model, BOM, cost and validation.

 Maturity
 CONCEPT, ENGINEERED, VALIDATED, PROFESSIONAL REVIEWED where applicable, FIELD-BUILT and FIELD-VERIFIED.

 Adaptation
 Parametric adaptation is allowed only within declared ranges; substitutions or major feature changes trigger affected revalidation or
 a new layout problem.

 Style
 Engineering templates and interior style packages are separate.

 Learning
 Field/as-built evidence may create reviewed new template versions but cannot silently rewrite published templates.



 13. Layout Recommendation & Constraint Solver

 Strategy
 Match verified templates first, adapt parametrically second, combine validated modules where permitted, and generate new
 constrained layouts only when necessary.

 Constraints
 Hard constraints include shell/protected zones, critical rules, fixed SKU dimensions, mass/safety limits and Must Haves. Soft
 constraints include preferences and optional features.

 Ranking
 Requirement fit, buildability, serviceability, comfort, storage, weight distribution, utility feasibility, cost, spatial efficiency and
 evidence confidence.

 Infeasibility
 Explain limiting constraints and offer alternatives without silently dropping Must Haves.

 Fail-safe
 Solver jobs are bounded/checkpointed and valid partial candidates survive later failures.



 14. Visualization & Interaction
 2D
 Top, left/right elevations, front/rear, roof, underfloor, longitudinal and cross-sections are first-class views.

 3D
 Recognizable donor exterior and dimensionally correct interior share canonical coordinates with 2D.

 Layers
 Structure, furniture, storage, electrical, solar, fresh/grey/black water, HVAC, insulation, roof, underfloor, clearances and human
 envelopes.

 Cutaway
 Transparent/cutaway modes reveal hidden systems and connection tracing.

 Customization
 Finishes, upholstery, flooring, panels, ceiling, cabinets, lighting and trim update appearance plus relevant mass/cost.

 Graceful degradation
 Renderer failure cannot disable canonical CAD or destroy project state.



 15. Validation & Quality Gates
 Pipeline
 Geometry > structure > motion/ergonomics > MEP > mass/axles > electrical > water > HVAC > serviceability > compatibility >
 budget > regulatory.

 Independent status
 Each domain reports PASS, CONDITIONAL/WARNING, FAIL or VERIFICATION REQUIRED as appropriate; no misleading single
 green tick.

 Change impact
 Dependency tracking invalidates and reruns only affected checks.

 Reproducibility
 Reports retain rule, inputs, calculation, result, severity, timestamp, versions, evidence and assumptions.

 Blocking
 Critical failures block Validated and Design Frozen states.

 16. Evidence, Provenance & Truth
 Classes
 Authoritative fact, verified measurement, derived, community-measured, estimated and unknown.

 Lineage
 Source, date, applicability, method, units, tolerance, confidence, formulas/inputs, reviewer and supersession.

 Conflicts
 Contradictory evidence creates a conflict record; never average or silently choose.

 AI boundary
 AI can discover/extract/propose candidate data but cannot directly promote consequential data to canonical truth.

 Propagation
 Source corrections identify affected twins, templates, calculations and projects.



 17. Reliability, Recovery & Fail-Safe Orchestrator
 Pattern
 Stable checkpoint > validate input > execute > checkpoint > validate output > atomic commit. Failure preserves last valid state, then
 bounded retry/fallback/resume/rollback.

 Controls
 Autosave, immutable revisions, undo/redo, idempotency, timeouts, retry/backoff, health checks, circuit breakers, partial-result
 preservation, crash journal and resource guards.

 Isolation
 3D failure leaves CAD intact; AI failure leaves deterministic engineering intact; regulatory outage leaves project accessible but fresh
 compliance unavailable; stale price data is labeled.

 Migration
 Schema migrations require checkpoint/backup and rollback on failure.

 Diagnostics
 Human-readable error plus technical diagnostic/correlation record and recovery action.



 18. Project Lifecycle, Design Freeze & Change Control
 Stages
 Draft > Concept > Layout Candidate > Layout Approved > Engineering > Services Routed > Validation Pending > Validated >
 Design Frozen > Fabrication Package > Under Construction > As-Built.

 Gates
 Stage transitions require defined checks and unresolved-item rules.

 Freeze
 Capture canonical model/twin versions, components, services/routes, calculations, cost, regulatory snapshot and validation report.

 Change control
 Post-freeze consequential edits create controlled revisions/change requests and impact analysis.

 History
 Frozen baselines are immutable, diffable and recoverable.

 20. As-Built Digital Twin & Lifecycle
 Commissioning
 Capture actual installed components, measured positions/dimensions, tank capacities, battery/inverter/solar, protection values,
 wiring/plumbing routes, access panels, seating/storage and final mass/axle data where available.

 Deviation
 Frozen design and As-Built remain distinct. Construction deviations are explicit and trigger affected revalidation.

 Maintenance
 Serviceable components retain location, service/repair/replacement history and access information; hidden-system locator traces
 actual routes.

 Upgrades
 Future changes are evaluated against the As-Built Twin, not the original concept.

 Lifecycle
 Future modifications become new projects based on the latest As-Built version. Technical records may transfer with the vehicle
 while personal data remains separate.

 Feedback
 Field evidence may improve future templates only through controlled review/versioning.

 End-to-End Product Flow
 User intent -> jurisdiction/private-commercial -> acquisition path -> budget -> exact donor -> regulatory eligibility -> vehicle digital
 twin -> structured requirements -> verified-template search -> parametric adaptation/constraint solving -> hidden-service packaging
 -> engineering and cost validation -> multi-view 2D -> synchronized accurate 3D -> appearance customization -> final validation ->
 design freeze -> fabrication package -> construction -> As-Built Digital Twin -> maintenance/upgrades/future modifications.


 Implementation invariant
 There is only one RV. 2D sees it. 3D sees it. Electrical and plumbing see it. Engineering, costing, regulation and fabrication
 documentation see it. The final As-Built Twin inherits from the same canonical lineage.
