     Key Highlights of Karnataka Tourism Policy 2024-29
     The policy aims to create a suitable tourism ecosystem for all segments of society and increase tourism’s contribution towards
     the $1 Trillion Economy for Karnataka
    The Policy is based on 9 Strategic Pillars that provide a strong foundation for a holistic and inclusive tourism ecosystem
