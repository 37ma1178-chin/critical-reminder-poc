> Copied from the project chat as delivered (text chat, 21 Nov 2026 IST). Reproduced from the chat text visible to the packager; wording kept as delivered.

These are the interior sizes used inside each 3D camper model, in millimetres. Most of them are my planning estimates, built from the manufacturer's outside dimensions. Only the Tata Winger's inside height and floor height are published by the manufacturer. The "Source" column says which numbers are which. A dash means that item isn't in the design.

**How to read the aisle column:** the first number is the narrowest point, beside the dinette. The others are the wider gaps further along, next to the wardrobe, bathroom or sofa.

## Things to know
- **Bed sizes:** the rear bed size is the platform. The mattress is 60 mm smaller each way.
- **Water tank:** 173 litres on every van and the LP 410, and 630 litres on the two long buses.
- **Too-tight aisles:** the 50 mm gap beside the dinette on the standard Traveller and the Winger is too narrow to walk through. On those narrow vans the dinette needs to be slimmer or moved. I'll raise this in the design review.
- **Not modelled:** the wheel arches inside are left out, and the walls are assumed to be 40 mm thick.
- **Force's published cargo sizes:** Force's delivery-van leaflet lists cargo spaces of 2820, 3300, 3950 and 4653 mm long by 1680 wide by 1830 high. Those are panel vans with a different body, so they're only a rough check: the real inside width is probably narrower than my estimate of 1845. Source: [Force delivery van leaflet](https://poomkudy.com/userdata/motorsforce/20210218_013400_542622.pdf). Force publishes no inside sizes for the Urbania.
- **Tata Winger source:** [Tata Winger 12S](https://busesandvans.tatamotors.com/product/tata-winger-12s).
