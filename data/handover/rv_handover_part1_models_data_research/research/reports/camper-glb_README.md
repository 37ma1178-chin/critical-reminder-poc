## What is in every file (node names)

- `Exterior_Body`: `Body_Shell` (opaque: right wall, front, rear, floor pan) + `Body_Cutaway_LeftWall_Roof` (semi-transparent left/kerb-side wall and roof, alpha 0.18, so the interior shows; hide this node to open the cutaway), windscreen, side/rear glazing, accent stripe, door frame (left, kerb side), lamps, grille, bumpers, mirrors, `Inlet_230V_Shore_Power_IP44` on the left rear.
- `Chassis_Wheels`: wheels at the published wheelbase (front axle = front overhang back from the nose), dual rear wheels where the OEM lists 6 tyres, axles, chassis rails, `Grey_Water_Tank_Underfloor`.
- `Cab`: driver seat on the right (RHD), co-driver seat, dashboard, steering wheel, `Fire_Extinguisher_1_2kg_Driver` beside the driver seat.
- `Interior_AIS124_Living`: floor, fixed transverse rear double bed over a storage garage, `Water_Tank_Fresh_<litres>L`, battery (1x, buses 4x LiFePO4) + `Electrical_Panel_MCB_Inverter_Charger` + `Wiring_Conduit_230V_IS2465`, kitchenette (base cabinets, countertop, 2-burner cooktop, stainless sink, tap; 12 V fridge on longer bodies), dinette (two facing benches with storage bases + removable table; converts to a second bed), overhead lockers where internal height >= 1.75 m, wardrobe or lounge sofa, wet room (cassette toilet, shower tray, basin) on Traveller 4020 / Wider Body / Traveller 26 / Urbania 4400 and all three buses, `Fire_Extinguisher_2_2kg_Kitchen_Door` near the entry door/kitchen.
- Materials are simple named PBR base colours (e.g. Paint_Body_White, Cabinet_Birch_Ply, Upholstery_Fabric_Blue, Water_Tank_Blue, Fire_Extinguisher_Red). No textures.

AIS-124 items covered: seats + table, sleeping accommodation, cooking facilities, storage, all rigidly fixed (table removable); 2 x 2 kg extinguishers with one near the driver (clause 3.4); 230 V input (clause 4). Chassis, suspension, brakes, fuel system and engine are not altered in any model (clause 0.1).

## Coverage decisions

- Force Traveller N passenger range on forcemotors.com: 3050WB, 3350WB, Wider Body 3350WB (5615 x 2245 x 2720, GVW 4125; added 19 Nov 2026), 3700WB, 4020WB, Wider Body 4020WB (all built). Also built: Traveller 26 (4020WB, 2225 wide, 2870 high with AC) from a 2018 BS-IV brochure: a legacy model, not on the current Force site; kept as built earlier. 7 Traveller GLBs, 14 GLBs in total.
- Force Urbania: 3350WB, 3615WB, 4400WB (all built; parent-supplied figures from Force Urbania pages; 2550 mm height, full stand-up).
- Tata Winger passenger, current (busesandvans.tatamotors.com): 3488WB High Roof 12S/15S (5458-5478 x 1905 x 2670, internal height 1900) -> built (one GLB covers 12S/15S/9S HR AC). Excluded: 3200WB Flat Roof (FL 3200 FR 9S/12S/13S, 4940 x 1905 x 2110 per Tata school-van brochure) - low roof, not stand-up; 3488WB non-AC high roof listed 2490 (school brochure) / 2409 (Winger Plus non-AC, per parent research) - under the ~2.6 m stand-up threshold. School 20+A+D and ambulance Wingers are not passenger tourist variants.
- Buses (coach-built motorhome body on an incomplete type-approved chassis, chassis systems untouched): Tata LP 410 3400, Eicher Pro 3010 L, Ashok Leyland Viking 5334. Ashok Leyland Viking length: OEM 10934 mm used as the planning length; conflict with TrucksBuses 10699 mm (FOH 2265, ROH 3100) is unresolved in donors.json. Body heights 2800 / 3200 / 3400 mm are estimates (donors.json proposedBody).
- Excluded (not built): Mahindra Bolero MaXX Pik-Up HD and Mahindra Supro Van, per instruction. Note: the LP 410, Eicher and Viking chassis, like any goods-category N base, are not directly covered by the AIS-124 'motor caravan' definition (category M); the three bus chassis here are M-category bus chassis, but the bodies remain concepts until a test agency (ARAI/ICAT) type-approves a real build.

## Limitations

- Body shapes are simplified (flat-sided prisms with a sloped nose/windscreen); no curved panels, door cut lines or real styling. Van overhangs (except Traveller 26 and the buses), floor heights (except Winger 495), cab lengths, ground clearance for Urbania, Urbania tyre size, and the LP 410 / Viking tyre sizes are estimates. Track width is approximated from body width minus tyre width.
- Living layouts are fitted to estimated internal boxes (body minus 40 mm walls), not measured interiors; wheel-arch intrusion is not modelled inside. Seats in the cab were moved back to clear the dashboard, so the effective cab zone is ~0.2 m longer than the donors.json cab estimate.
- Weights, payload and axle loads were not checked (mostly unknown in the seed).
