Eicher Pro 3010 L Chassis

The all-new Eicher Pro 3010 L chassis is built strong with domex® material, silent and low maintenance parabolic suspension with Anti roll bar, radial tyres and many other advance features making it an ideal options for any application. BSVI chassis is driven by state-of-the-art E494 engine and ET40S6 transmission which provides powerful performance. Its superior fuel efficiency coupled with lower maintenance cost leads to high profitability for its stakeholders.
