Questionnaire as JSON

The same format as data/questionnaires/*.json, so it runs with node tools/eligibility.js. You can paste a questionnaire here and load it into the form.

How to read a verdict. Eligible: every rule that can be checked passes, and the furniture fits at the unfavourable end of every dimension's error band. Undecided: the answer depends on an estimated or unknown dimension, an open legal question, or a document that is missing. Rejected: a legal rule or conversion gate fails, or the items do not fit even at the favourable end. Space is a two-lane screening model, not a drawn layout.
